What can Sci-JEPA do?

Sci-JEPA reads research as structured parts (the problem, the method, the conditions, the result, a claim and its evidence). Give it some parts and it predicts the representation of a missing one, then ranks real findings from about two million arXiv papers by how well they fit that prediction. Every answer links to the paper it came from.

How to read the answers

  • Answers are published findings that fit the model's prediction. They are not generated text and not a fact check.
  • The score mixes the model's prediction (jepa), text similarity (text) and keyword overlap (bm25). Scores compare answers within one request only. Under Options you can rank by one of them alone and see the difference.
  • Papers are tagged train, val or test: the split they were in when the model was trained.
  • Every panel has Show the API call: the same request as curl or Python, and the raw answer.

Ask in plain words

Describe your research in a few sentences. The server splits the text into parts, asks for whatever the task still needs, then answers. Use English, at least two sentences.

Check a claim

Give a claim and the evidence for it. The model's relation head says whether the evidence supports or challenges the claim, and lists related evidence from the corpus on each side.

What if…?

Describe a study, then change one part, for example another method or another dataset. The model predicts the result before and after the change, says how far the prediction moved, and shows the findings that fit each version.

Spot an edit

Give a finding and a copy with one thing changed (a flipped direction, a negation, another number, a swapped term). Does the model tell them apart? Add the study's context to use the rule from the evaluation: does the model's prediction prefer the original?

Explore papers

Search the corpus by topic (the index has no titles yet, so search ranks papers by content) or by arXiv ID. Open a paper to see its knowledge units, the parts the model reads. Then hide one part and see whether the model finds it again.
